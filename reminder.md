## My Design Philosophy

dark, minimal interface that feels refined, precise, and thoughtfully engineered, much like a well-designed car.

- Minimalism Keep the interface clean and purposeful. Every element should have a reason to exist.
- Subtle color: Use a mostly monochromatic palette, with small accents of color to draw attention where needed.
- Smooth motion: Use restrained animations and transitions that feel natural and responsive, never distracting.
- Readability Prioritize clear typography, comfortable spacing, and strong contrast.
- Attention to detail Keep borders, alignment, spacing, and interactions consistent throughout the experience.
